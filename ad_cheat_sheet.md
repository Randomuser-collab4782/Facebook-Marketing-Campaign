## CANVA DESIGN TIPS

### Template Approach
1. Go to **canva.com** → Create Design → **"Instagram Post (Square)"** (1080×1080)
2. Set background to Navy `#1E3A5F`
3. Add your headline in **Poppins Bold**, colour White
4. Add subtext in **Inter Regular**, colour `#F9FAFB`
5. Add a CTA button: rounded rectangle in Amber `#F6AE2D` with dark text
6. Keep text to under 20% of the image area (Meta penalises text-heavy images)

### Design Variations to Test
| Style              | Background       | Text Colour | Accent      | Mood               |
|-------------------|------------------|-------------|-------------|---------------------|
| Clean Modern      | Navy `#1E3A5F`   | White       | Teal        | Professional trust  |
| Student Lifestyle | Bright photo     | Navy overlay| Amber       | Energetic, relatable|
